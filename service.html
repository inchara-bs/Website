Our Services


🍽️ In-Store & Daily Services
• Dine-In & Counter Service: Quick-service model for walk-in customers looking for freshly prepared, instant street food plates.
• Takeaway & Packaging: Specially designed, leak-proof, and separate component packaging (e.g., packing puris, dry fillings, and flavored waters separately) to ensure the chaat doesn't get soggy before getting home.
• Online Food Delivery: Integration with aggregators (Swiggy, Zomato) for doorstep delivery, featuring optimized combo meals and family packs.

🎪 Live Event Catering Services
• Live Street Carts: Authentic, brightly lit wooden or metal carts designed to bring a traditional "Mela" or "Chowk" market vibe directly to the venue.
• Live Pani Puri Counter: An interactive station featuring automated tap dispensers or traditional earthen pots filled with 3 to 5 customized water flavors (e.g., Teekha Pudina, Khatta Meetha, Hing, Garlic, and Lemon-Ginger).
• Hot Tawa (Griddle) Station: On-site, live frying and searing for hot items like Aloo Tikki, Pav Bhaji, Ragda Patties, and Chole Bhature.
• Custom Customization Counter: A interactive setup where guests can pick their precise spice, yogurt (dahi), and chutney levels for plates like Papdi Chaat, Sev Puri, and Bhel Puri.
