About Us

Every great business starts with a problem, but mine started with a full-blown existential crisis at 2:30 AM during last semester’s finals week.
I was buried under a mountain of textbooks, staring at a laptop screen that was burning my retinas, and trying to memorize concepts that felt entirely detached from reality. My brain was completely fried. I was running on four hours of sleep, and my stomach was growling. I reached into my backpack and pulled out what had become my fuel for the past week: a dry, cardboard-textured protein bar and a lukewarm mug of bitter instant coffee.
I looked at that sad excuse for a meal and thought, "Is this it? Is this what the prime years of our youth are supposed to taste like?"
Being a student is an incredibly intense, stressful, and let's be honest, profoundly bland experience. Day after day, we are forced to consume rigid formulas, heavy academic jargon, and endless deadlines. Our minds are working overtime, but our daily lives become entirely monotonous, painted in shades of library carpet and fluorescent classroom lights.
That night, a desperate, overwhelming craving hit me. I didn't want fuel; I wanted an experience. I wanted something loud. I wanted crunch, spice, tang, fire, and sweetness all exploding in one single, chaotic bite. I needed a plate of authentic, soul-satisfying chat.
But at 2:30 AM in a college town, options are non-existent. Everything was locked down. As I sat there dreaming of the perfect sev puri, a lightbulb went off. I realized that my classmates and I didn't just need a place to buy quick food between lectures. We needed a place to wake up. We needed a sanctuary where we could shock our senses back to life.
So, against all practical advice about "focusing strictly on my studies," I decided to open this shop.
I wanted to build a vibrant hub that was the exact antithesis of a quiet, suffocating exam hall. I envisioned a space filled with the rhythmic clinking of metal spoons against plates, the intoxicating aroma of roasted cumin drifting through the air, and a colorful counter stacked with fresh chutneys and crispy snacks.
Now, balanced between attending morning lectures and managing inventory, I get to run this shop. Every time I crush a crispy, hollow puri, pack it with spiced potatoes, and splash that bright, tangy tamarind chutney, I don't feel like a store owner—I feel like a chemist mixing the ultimate antidote to student burnout.
We didn't just open a food stall to survive the student budget. We created a daily escape pod. It is a place where you can pause the academic stress, laugh with your friends, and experience a total explosion of flavor. We are serving up happiness, one chaotic, delicious plate at a time.
